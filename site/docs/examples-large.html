Examples

Large patchbays

Two patchbays of two dozen rules each, showing how the small forms compose into a whole conditioning layer: JSON demux at the head, stable dashboard channels, safety alerts, roll-ups, liveness and audit trails.

Both files live in advanced-examples/. They were written by a coding agent with AGENTS_PATCHBAY.md loaded, for deliberately fictional domains. They are not reference deployments or domain-accurate models, but they validate, they run, and they give a feel for what is possible. Each has a matching Python pump script that publishes randomised telemetry.

How a large patchbay is laid out#

Both files follow the same order, and it is a good template for your own:

  1. Cache. One (lvc ...) form opts the input, alert, ops and audit trees into the last-value cache, so a dashboard that connects late is populated at once.
  2. Demux. One :reentrant true rule per JSON frame type breaks the frame into scalar subjects. Everything after this works on plain numbers and strings.
  3. Stable channels. Short -> pipelines (smooth, round, gate, publish) give dashboards quiet subjects to subscribe to.
  4. Liveness. sample! republishes the latest frame on a fixed cadence and dropout flags a rig that has gone silent.
  5. Alerts. transition over a windowed statistic raises one alert and one all-clear; rising-edge plus throttle caps one-shot alerts.
  6. Roll-ups. aggregate!, bar! and count! produce the numbers a report wants.
  7. Audit. Rules build dated subjects with subject-with and now, so events land on subjects like audit.2026-11-11.rig-03.seal.
  8. Fleet meter. One rule on the whole tree publishes the ingest rate.

Moonwell#

Advanced A fictional lunar ice-mining habitat. Rigs publish three nested JSON frames (env, drill, power) and free-form log lines under moonwell.<rig>.*.

Forms worth studying here:

ruletechnique
moonwell.*.env demuxjson-demux! with [PATH SUFFIX] pairs flattens nested paths such as sensor.thermal.temp to a short suffix (temp)
env.temp stablea time window feeding value gates: moving-avg :ms 45000, then round, then squelch
env livenesstwo effects in one do: sample! for a 5-second snapshot and dropout for stale and recovered
env.dust alerta boolean threaded through rising-edge then throttle :ms 300000 2: at most two dust alerts in five minutes
env.radiation alerttransition over a 95th percentile, so one outlier does not raise the alarm
env.seal auditchanged? as a predicate, publishing to a dated subject built with subject-with and (now :date)
drill.torque chatterspread over a window: moving-max minus moving-min
drill.modevector membership with contains?, a count! and an alert in one do
env.icethree clock forms on one subject: debounce!, aggregate! and a one-minute bar!
moonwell.> meterrate :ms 1000 across the whole tree for a fleet-wide messages-per-second figure

Cloudhook#

Advanced A fictional offshore fleet of autonomous kite turbines. Kites publish flat JSON frames for flight, weather and power under cloudhook.<kite>.*.

The structure matches Moonwell, with a few different techniques:

  • Compound conditions in a transition. The altitude and voltage rules use (or (< avg LO) (> avg HI)) to alert when a smoothed value leaves a band in either direction. Each moving-avg inside the or is its own call site with its own ring.
  • Operator commands as subjects. The tether and shear rules publish to ops.<kite>.winch.ease and ops.<kite>.pattern.widen: the alert is addressed to whatever acts on it.
  • Integer payloads. (= payload-int 1) through rising-edge records the moment a brake engages, once.
  • Quantized bands. moving-avg, quantize 10, squelch reports altitude in 10-unit bands, so a dashboard updates only when the kite changes band.

The pump scripts default to 127.0.0.1:4222. Use --host and --port if monoblok is listening somewhere else.

Sizing a large patchbay#

  • Cost follows the rules that match a message, not the number of rules in the file. Narrow filters keep the per-message work small.
  • Each re-entrant demux adds one round of rule evaluation per emitted field. Demux only the fields that later rules or subscribers use.
  • Every stateful form keeps a slot per concrete subject. A wildcard over thousands of devices is thousands of slots; see Budgeting bar state for how to measure it.
  • (now :minute) in a subject creates a new subject every minute. Prefer :hour or :date unless you mean it.
